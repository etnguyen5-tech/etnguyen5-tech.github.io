Tech Heroes

Hello, this is Group 6 and these are our Tech Heroes! These are people we have personally been inspired by as they have worked towards challenging norms in the tech space. Read more about each of our heroes below!

  • Aarnavi's Tech Hero: Reshma Saujani

    My tech hero is Reshma Saujani because of her strength and ability to pave the way for young girls in the 21st century to break into coding. Saujani is the founder of Girls Who Code and Moms First, and she labels herself as a leading activist who advocates for closing the gender gap in technology and empowers women. She has given TED Talks and is also a New York Times bestselling author of several books. As a brown woman myself, I'm inspired by the fact that she is a brown leading woman in the tech industry!
    Click here to learn more about Reshma Saujani!
  • Shivani's Tech Hero: Timnit Gebru

    Timnit Gebru is my tech hero because she calls out bias in AI and pushes for more people of color in the field. She co-founded Black in AI and started DAIR, an independent institute that studies how AI affects marginalized communities. If you map out everyone connected to an AI system, there are the people the data comes from, the experts who build it, the people it makes decisions about, and the decision makers who use it. Usually only the experts and decision makers get a say, but DAIR brings in the people who are actually affected.
    Click here to learn more about DAIR!
  • Eva's Tech Hero: Susan Kare

    Susan Kare is my tech hero because she showed how graphic design can play an important role in technology. She is best known for designing many of the original icons and visual elements for the Apple Macintosh, making technology easier and more intuitive for users. I admire her because her work combines creativity, design, and technology, which are all areas I am passionate about. As someone interested in UX/UI and graphic design, I want to create digital experiences that are not only visually appealing but also simple, accessible, and easy to understand. Susan Kare inspires me to see design as more than just making something look good—it can also improve how people interact with and experience technology.
