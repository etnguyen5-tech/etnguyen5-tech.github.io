Implicit Bias Resources

Implicit bias is the attitudes and stereotypes we have without realizing it, and they can affect how we treat people.

→ Curious where you stand? Take the Implicit Association Test (IAT) from Harvard!

What is Intersectionality?

Intersectionality is the idea that race, gender, class, and other parts of who we are overlap, so you can't understand someone's experience by looking at just one.

→ Kimberlé Crenshaw's TED Talk, The Urgency of Intersectionality

Our Take

The IAT shows that the associations are there, but not how much they affect what you do. It also only tests one thing at a time, like race or gender, so even if you take them all, you get separate scores instead of seeing how they cross.

Questions to Think About

  • When you picture a programmer or engineer, who do you picture?
  • When a tech company says it's "diverse," who is it actually counting?
